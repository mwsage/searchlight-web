Common questions

What is a Searchlight?

It is the signal that something is happening now. You throw one up from a group you are in, add what is happening and where, and everyone in that group gets the heads up. When it is over, you turn it off.

How do I invite someone?

Share your group's link, or show them the QR code. If they do not have the app yet, the link takes them to a page that explains what they were invited to and how to get it.

Why can only some groups be shared by link?

Link sharing is a public-group feature. In a private group the owner decides who is in it, and a link that anyone can forward would take that decision away from them. Private groups are invited person by person on purpose.

Where did my event go?

Events are meant to be temporary. When a Searchlight is turned off the event and its chat stop appearing — the group stays, the plan does not. That is the design, not a bug.

How do I turn off analytics?

Settings → the analytics toggle. See the privacy policy for what is collected and what is not.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. It removes your account and the personal data attached to it, and it cannot be undone. If you cannot reach that screen for any reason, email us and we will do it for you.

I am having trouble signing in.

Try the password-reset link on the sign-in screen first. If you signed up with Google or Apple, use that same button rather than a password — the account is tied to it. If you are still stuck, email us with the address or phone number you signed up with.

Reporting something serious

If you have seen behaviour in a group that should not be there, or you believe an account belongs to a child under 13, email support@searchlight.social and say so in the subject line. Those get looked at first.
