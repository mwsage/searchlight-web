Searchlight

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 August 2026

Searchlight is an app for letting a group of people you already know that something is happening. This policy describes what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. It describes the app as it is actually built — not what we might add later.

What Searchlight collects

Information you give us

What Why
Email address, or phone number if you sign in with a phone To create and sign you into your account
Profile details — display name, handle, bio, profile photo, and any social media handles you choose to add So the people in your groups know who you are. All of it is optional except a name and handle.
Your groups and events — group names and descriptions, the events you create, and messages you send in an event's chat This is the app working. Group members can see it.

Information collected automatically

What Why
An account identifier and, if you allow notifications, a device push token To keep your data associated with your account and to deliver the notification when someone in your group starts something
Anonymous usage events — a short, fixed list of product actions such as "a group was created" or "an invite was accepted" To understand whether the app works. These are not linked to your account — see below.
Crash and performance diagnostics To find and fix bugs. Not linked to your account, and no personal information is attached.

Analytics are not tied to you. Searchlight's analytics run on an anonymous identifier generated on your device. The app never sends your account id, email, phone number or name to its analytics provider, so usage events cannot be traced back to your account. You can also turn analytics off entirely in Settings.

Location

Where you say something is happening is text you typed, not a reading from your device. Typing "the lounge at 7" shares no location data at all, and that is how Searchlight works by default.

Separately, Searchlight contains an optional live location sharing feature for an event you created. It is off unless you turn it on, it only ever applies to your own events, and while it is on your device's coordinates are sent periodically so the group can find you — stopping when the event ends or when you turn it off. Searchlight never collects your location in the background, and never for an event someone else created.

Being precise about this: as of this version the control to switch live location sharing on is not present in the app, so in practice it does not run. We describe the capability rather than omitting it, because the code ships in the app and you deserve to know what it can do — not only what today's screens expose.

What Searchlight does not collect

  • Advertising identifiers. There is no IDFA, no ad tracking, and no tracking of you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your contacts. Searchlight does not read your address book. You invite people by sharing a link.
  • Background location. Nothing about Searchlight tracks where you are when you are not using it.

Who else sees your information

Other people in Searchlight. Your profile is visible to people in your groups. Anything you post in a group or an event chat is visible to the members of that group. A public group can be found and joined by anyone with its link.

Service providers who run the app for us. Searchlight uses a small number of companies to operate. They process data on our instructions and for no purpose of their own:

  • Google Firebase — accounts and sign-in, the database that stores your groups, events and messages, file storage for photos, and push notifications.
  • PostHog — the anonymous product analytics described above.
  • Sentry — crash and performance diagnostics.

Nobody else. Searchlight does not sell your information, does not share it with advertisers, and does not trade it. If we are ever legally compelled to disclose something, we will do so only to the extent required.

How long it is kept

Your account, profile and groups are kept for as long as your account exists.

An honest note about events. When a Searchlight ends, the event and its chat stop appearing in the app — that is the product working the way it is meant to. It is not the same as erasure: those records remain stored on your account until you delete your account, at which point they go with it. We would rather say this plainly than let "the events disappear" imply something the database does not do.

Your choices

  • Delete your account. Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and the personal data attached to it. It cannot be undone.
  • Turn off analytics. Settings → the analytics toggle. The app stops sending usage events.
  • Turn off notifications. Your device's system settings, at any time.
  • Edit or remove your profile details. Anything optional — photo, bio, social handles — can be changed or cleared in the app.
  • Leave a group. You can leave any group you are in.

If you want a copy of your data, or you have a request this list does not cover, email support@searchlight.social and we will handle it directly.

Children

Searchlight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored with our service providers under access rules that restrict who can read what. No service can promise perfect security, and we will not pretend otherwise — but access to your groups and messages is enforced on the server, not merely hidden in the app.

Changes to this policy

If what the app collects changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top is updated. Check the date above to see whether anything has changed since you last read it.
