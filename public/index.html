Built for real-life coordination

Searchlight is made for the plans that are too casual for a calendar invite and too real to leave to chance.

  • A cigar at the lounge.
  • A round after work.
  • A drink downtown.
  • A bonfire tonight.
  • A last-minute game.
  • A quick, "I'm heading here — who's in?"

It is not another feed. It is not another place to perform. It is not another social network asking for your whole life.

It's a beacon.

How it works

  1. 1

    Create your group

    Start a group around the an interest, geography, or activity.

    • Your cigar crew.
    • Your golf group.
    • Your neighborhood friends.
    • Your Saturday regulars.
  2. 2

    Bring your people

    Copy the group's invite link and text it to them.

    They tap it, install the app, and land straight in your group — the link remembers which one.

    • In person? Show them the group's QR code.
    • Nothing to search for. Nothing to look up.
    • Works whether or not they have heard of Searchlight.
  3. 3

    Throw up a Searchlight

    When something is happening, send the signal.

    Add the what, when, and where. Keep it loose or make it specific.

  4. 4

    Your group is notified

    The group gets the heads up.

    Quick RSVP, chat inside the event.

    Coordinate while the event is live.

The chat lasts as long as the plan does.

Every Searchlight opens a thread just for that event. Coordinate who's in, when, and where to meet.

When the plan's over, the thread goes with it.

  • No group chat that never stops buzzing.
  • No 200 messages to scroll past tomorrow.
  • No muting the people you actually like.

The conversation is about tonight. Then it's gone.

No number required.

You don't trade phone numbers to add someone. Point them at your shared-interest group and they join.

  • Scan a QR code when you're standing together.
  • Or send a link — in any chat, to one person or the whole crew.
  • It opens the group to join. New here? The same link offers the download first.

No number to ask for. No awkward exchange.

Made for people who want to do something... WITH their friends

Most social tools are used to show your friends what you did, not what you're doing right now.

Searchlight is different.

It is built for the person who says, "I'm heading to the lounge around 7," and wants the group to know without personally texting twelve people.

It's for lightweight coordination that actually gets people together.

Less planning. More showing up.

Life gets busy. Friendships get passive. Good plans die because nobody wants to be the organizer.

Searchlight makes the ask smaller.

  • You are not hosting an event.

  • You are not asking people to commit.

You are just lighting the signal.

Showing up is good for you. Really.

The Harvard Study of Adult Development followed 724 men for over 80 years. Its clearest finding: the ones with the strongest relationships lived longer, healthier, happier lives — more than money, more than fame, more than genes.

A review of 148 studies and 300,000 people found it from the other side: strong social ties raise your odds of survival by about 50% — on par with quitting smoking.

Friendship isn't a nice-to-have. It's one of the best things you can do for your life.

Searchlight exists to make the showing-up part easy.

Sources: Harvard Study of Adult Development · Holt-Lunstad et al., PLoS Medicine, 2010
