Reporting content and blocking people

Every place someone else's content appears, you can report it, and you can block the person who posted it. Both are in the app, not behind an email.

Reporting

Press and hold a message in an event to report that message. Open a person's profile to report the person. You pick a reason — Spam, Harassment, Hate, Sexual content, Violence, or Other — and can add a note. The report goes to us with the message or profile attached, so we can see what you saw.

Blocking

Open a person's profile and block them. A block is immediate and it works in both directions: their messages stop appearing in your event threads, they disappear from the member lists you see, and neither of you can reach the other. Your blocked list is in Settings → Blocked accounts, where you can unblock someone if you change your mind.

What we do about a report

We review reports and act on them within 24 hours. Acting means removing the content, ejecting the person who posted it, or both. If a report is about something that puts someone in immediate danger, we involve law enforcement.

If you would rather write to a person than use the in-app report, email support@searchlight.social and say what happened. Reports about a child under 13, or about anything sexual involving a minor, are looked at first.
